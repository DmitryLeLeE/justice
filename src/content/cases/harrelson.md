## The case

Federal Judge John H. Wood Jr., nicknamed "Maximum John", was killed outside his San Antonio home. Charles Harrelson was tried for the killing, together with Jo Ann Harrelson and Elizabeth Chagra, according to the Library of Congress record of the trial.

The trial was heard in Houston in 1982, before Judge William S. Sessions according to the title of the drawing. Gary Myrick sketched it in colored pencil on purple-gray paper. Harrelson was convicted on 14 December 1982, and his final appeal was rejected by the Supreme Court in 2004.

## The crime

Judge Wood, known as "Maximum John" for his heavy sentences in drug cases, was the first federal judge killed in the 20th century. He was shot with a rifle outside his home in San Antonio, and he had been scheduled to try the drug dealer Jamiel Chagra.

## In the courtroom

The government said Chagra's family had paid for the killing. Charles Harrelson, a professional gambler and former hit man, was tied to the murder, and his wife Jo Ann Harrelson and Elizabeth Chagra, Jamiel's wife, were charged with conspiracy. Gary Myrick's drawing shows the trial in Houston, away from San Antonio where the killing took place.

## Aftermath

Harrelson was convicted of murder, and the two women of conspiracy. He died in a federal prison in 2007. His son, the actor Woody Harrelson, has spoken of the case in interviews.
