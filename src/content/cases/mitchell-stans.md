## The case

Former Attorney General John Mitchell and former Commerce Secretary Maurice Stans were tried in New York over a secret campaign contribution from financier Robert Vesco.

Both men were acquitted on 28 April 1974.

## In the courtroom

The government said Mitchell and Stans had used their positions to persuade the SEC to drop an inquiry into Vesco in exchange for a secret donation. The trial was the first in which the Nixon-era charges reached a jury,.

The case against the two men turned on John Dean, the White House counsel, whose credibility the defence attacked. The jury deliberated for a few days.

## Aftermath

The acquittal came as Watergate was closing in on the president. Mitchell did not escape for long: he was convicted in January 1975 for his part in the cover-up and served nineteen months.
