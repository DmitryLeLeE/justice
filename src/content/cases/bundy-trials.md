## The case

Between 1974 and 1978 Bundy killed young women across several states: Washington, Utah, Colorado and Florida. He was arrested in Utah in 1975 and convicted of kidnapping in 1976. In 1977 he escaped twice in Colorado. After the second escape, on 30 December, he went to Florida, where on 15 January 1978 he attacked four women in the Chi Omega sorority house at Florida State University, killing Margaret Bowman and Lisa Levy. A few weeks later, on 9 February, twelve-year-old Kimberly Leach was abducted from her junior high school in Lake City. He was caught again in mid-February.

## In the courtroom

The Chi Omega trial was moved to Miami and heard by Judge Edward Cowart. Bundy, who had studied law, dismissed several lawyers and largely represented himself in front of the cameras, which were allowed in the courtroom. The state's central evidence was a bite mark left on Lisa Levy, which a forensic dentist matched to casts of Bundy's teeth. He was convicted in July 1979 and sentenced to death for the two murders. The second trial, in Orlando, ended in February 1980 with a conviction for the murder of Kimberly Leach and a third death sentence. During it, Bundy married Carole Ann Boone in the courtroom.

## Aftermath

He spent nine years appealing while giving the police evasive confessions. In the days before his execution at Florida State Prison on 24 January 1989, he admitted to around thirty murders. Investigators think that the true number is higher.

## 18+ · The scale of it

Twenty killings are confirmed: seventeen before his arrest in 1975 and three after his second escape. Bundy confessed to thirty in the end, and investigators suspect more than thirty-six. His victims were mostly young women in their teens and early twenties. In the last days before the execution he gave recorded accounts, in graphic detail, of dozens of murders, among them that of Susan Curtis in Utah in June 1975. Margaret Bowman and Lisa Levy were beaten and strangled in their beds at the sorority house; two other women, Kathy Kleiner and Karen Chandler, survived the same night with severe injuries.
