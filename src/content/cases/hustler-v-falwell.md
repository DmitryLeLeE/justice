The Supreme Court heard Larry Flynt's appeal against a verdict for the Rev. Jerry Falwell, whom Hustler magazine had mocked in a parody advertisement.

On 24 February 1988 the Court ruled 8-0 that a public figure cannot recover damages for emotional distress from a parody.
