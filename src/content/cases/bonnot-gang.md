## The case

The Bonnot gang was among the first to use motor cars for robbery. Its first raid, in December 1911, was on a Société Générale branch in the 18th arrondissement of Paris. In March 1912 the gang stole a car in the forest of Sénart and killed the driver, and then robbed a bank at Chantilly where two cashiers were shot dead. Police numbers were raised and the bank offered a reward of 100,000 francs. Bonnot, the reputed leader, was killed in a siege; Octave Garnier was shot at a safe house.

## In the courtroom

The survivors came before the Seine assizes in Paris in February 1913. The courtroom sketch shown here by Delaroche is dated 8 February and shows Callemin, Metge and Soudy together.

## Aftermath

Callemin, Monier and Soudy were guillotined in April 1913.
