Edward S. Ellis was a deputy street superintendent in Los Angeles. In 1904 he stood trial on charges of accepting bribes to sign off on public works without inspecting them.

Everything this file records comes from the Commons description of the courtroom sketch: the charge, the hung jury and the mistrial on 31 December 1904, and the two women in the foreground, Ellis's wife and mother. It is a minor local case, included for the drawing.
