The Ligue des patriotes, founded by Paul Déroulède, was a nationalist league that backed General Boulanger in the late 1880s. As the government moved against Boulanger's movement in spring 1889, it also took its supporters to court.

Le Figaro printed a sketch of the hearing by Eugène Rapp on 3 April 1889. The League was ordered dissolved, though Déroulède returned to politics and later featured in the Dreyfus Affair.
