## The case

Musk was one of the founders of OpenAI and left its board in 2018. He said he had given about $44 million between 2016 and 2020 on the understanding that the company would stay a nonprofit working for the public benefit, and that it had broken the agreement by turning to profit. In April 2026 he amended his claim to ask that any damages go to OpenAI's charity and that Altman be removed from the board.

## In the courtroom

Jury selection began on 27 April 2026 in the Oakland courthouse, before Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers. Musk testified for three days and was questioned by his own lawyer, Steven Molo, and by OpenAI's William Savitt. Other witnesses included Jared Birchall, Shivon Zilis and the computer scientist Stuart Russell. The judge ruled that the question of existential risk from AI was outside the scope of the trial.

The drawing, by the artist Oeoi, is dated 13 May 2026.

## Aftermath

The jury found that Musk's claims came too late under the statute of limitations. The decision settled the case for the defendants without any ruling on the merits of the dispute.
