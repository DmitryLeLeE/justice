## The case

Robert Chambers was tried in New York for the 1986 killing of Jennifer Levin in Central Park, a case the tabloids called the Preppy Murder.

In March 1988, while the jury was still deliberating, Chambers pleaded guilty to manslaughter. He was sentenced to 5 to 15 years.

## In the courtroom

Chambers, 19, said that Levin had been the aggressor in a sexual encounter and that her death was an accident. The defence made her sexual history the heart of its case, which provoked anger and was widely criticised, and the tabloids called it the "Preppy Murder".

Marilyn Church drew the jury in March 1988.

## Aftermath

After days of deliberation the jury was split, and Chambers took the plea offered by the prosecutors: first-degree manslaughter, 5 to 15 years. He served the whole term. In 2008 he was convicted of selling drugs and received a sentence of 19 years.
