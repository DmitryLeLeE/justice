## The case

On 1 May 2010 a car loaded with an explosive device was left in Times Square in New York; it did not detonate. Faisal Shahzad was arrested days later and pleaded guilty in June 2010.

At the sentencing on 5 October 2010 he received a life sentence. Federal courts in the United States do not allow cameras, so the news media relied on artists to show the hearing to the public.

## In the courtroom

Shahzad, a naturalised American citizen born in Pakistan, pleaded guilty without a plea agreement and told the judge he had received bomb training in Pakistan. At sentencing he spoke at length about his motives. Judge Cedarbaum then passed the mandatory life term.

## Aftermath

The device in the Nissan Pathfinder had been poorly made, with fertiliser that would not detonate. A street vendor and a police officer noticed the smoke. The case was one of several where federal courts rather than military commissions handled terrorism suspects.
