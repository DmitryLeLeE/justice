The Senate voted 77 to 0 on 7 February 1973 to create a select committee to investigate the break-in at the Democratic National Committee offices in the Watergate complex and the efforts to conceal who was behind it. Senator Sam Ervin chaired it.

The public hearings opened on 17 May 1973. This was a hearing, not a criminal trial: it could expose facts and recommend reforms, but it could not convict anyone. Artists such as Joseph Papin sketched the senators and witnesses for the press.

The committee issued its final report on 27 June 1974, weeks before Richard Nixon resigned.
