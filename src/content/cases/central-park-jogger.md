Five teenagers were tried in New York for the 1989 rape and assault of a woman jogging in Central Park.

The five were convicted in 1990. Their convictions were vacated in 2002 after another man confessed and DNA evidence matched him.

The drawing shown is by Church, courtesy of the Library of Congress. The case file gives the main facts; see the sources for the full record.
