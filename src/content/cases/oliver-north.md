Lieutenant Colonel Oliver North was tried in Washington over his part in the Iran-Contra affair. Judge Gerhard Gesell presided.

North was convicted on three counts in May 1989. The convictions were later vacated on appeal and the charges dismissed.

The drawing shown is by Kenny, courtesy of the Library of Congress. The case file gives the main facts; see the sources for the full record.
