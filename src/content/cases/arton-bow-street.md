The collapse of the French Panama Canal company in 1889 led to a long political scandal over bribes paid to deputies and officials. Émile Arton, who had acted as an intermediary, left France to avoid prosecution.

He was arrested in London in 1895 and appeared on 21 November at Bow Street, where an artist for a French paper sketched the hearing.
