## The case

Philippe Monguillot, 58, was driving a Tram'Bus in Bayonne on 5 July 2020, at the height of the Covid restrictions. He asked a group of passengers to wear masks and show tickets. An argument followed, and he was beaten and left unconscious. He was declared brain-dead and treatment was stopped five days later.

## In the courtroom

The two main suspects were first charged with murder, then the charge was reduced to violence causing death without intent to kill. The trial took place before the assize court of the Pyrénées-Atlantiques in Pau. Prosecutors asked for fifteen years for each. After about four and a half hours of deliberation the court gave fifteen years to Manai and thirteen to Guyennon. A third man, accused of sheltering them, was tried separately.

## Aftermath

Bus drivers stopped work, security was increased on public transport, and around 6,000 people marched in Bayonne. The widow, Véronique Monguillot, said the sentences did not meet her demand for exemplary justice.
