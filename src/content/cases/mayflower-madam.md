## The case

Sydney Biddle Barrows, a society woman who ran a high-priced escort service in Manhattan, appeared in court on 2 July 1985.

Barrows pleaded guilty to promoting prostitution and was fined $5,000.

## The case

Barrows, a descendant of Mayflower passengers and a graduate of a fashion school, ran a high-priced escort agency from an office in Manhattan with a staff of educated young women, and the press called her the "Mayflower Madam". The case drew attention because of the clients, whose names were never released, and because she did not look like what a madam was supposed to be.

## In court

The prosecution accepted a plea. In July 1985 she pleaded guilty to a misdemeanour, promoting prostitution, and paid a $5,000 fine. Kenny drew her in court on the day of the plea. It was a case that ended without a trial or a sentence of any weight.

## Aftermath

Barrows wrote a bestselling memoir and a television film followed in 1987.
