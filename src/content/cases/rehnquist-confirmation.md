## The case

William Rehnquist testified before the Senate Judiciary Committee in 1971 on his nomination as an associate justice of the Supreme Court.

The Senate confirmed Rehnquist in December 1971. He became Chief Justice in 1986.

## In the room

Opponents asked about his positions on civil rights and civil liberties, and about a memorandum from his time as a law clerk at the Court in the early 1950s. Rehnquist said the memo reflected the views of the justice for whom he worked. He was confirmed in December 1971.

## Aftermath

Fifteen years later Nixon's appointee was raised to the Chief Justiceship by Ronald Reagan. He led the Court for almost nineteen years and presided over the Senate impeachment trial of Bill Clinton in 1999.
