## The case

Operation Family Secrets was a federal prosecution of alleged members and associates of the Chicago Outfit. The indictment tied the defendants to a series of murders dating back to the 1970s.

The trial took place in Chicago in 2007. Frank Calabrese Sr. was among the defendants, and members of his own family testified for the government. The sketch used here shows the prosecutor T. Markus Funk with Calabrese and Lombardo.

## In the courtroom

The government's best witnesses were men who had killed for the Outfit. Nicholas Calabrese, the defendant's brother, described his own part in a series of murders going back to the 1970s. Frank Calabrese Jr. testified against his father after he wore a recording device during visits to him in prison. The drawing here shows the prosecutor T. Markus Funk with Calabrese and Lombardo.

Lombardo, who had been a fugitive for almost two years before his arrest in 2007, was convicted of racketeering conspiracy and of the 1974 murder of Daniel Seifert.

## Aftermath

The convictions closed about eighteen killings, some of them nearly thirty years old, and are regarded as the biggest blow to the Chicago Outfit in decades. Calabrese Sr. died in prison in 2012.
