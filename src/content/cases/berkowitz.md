Between 1976 and 1977 a gunman shot a series of couples and young women in New York City, killing six people and writing letters to the press and police signed "Son of Sam". David Berkowitz was arrested in August 1977.

The case ended in a guilty plea in 1978 rather than a jury trial. Berkowitz repeatedly shouted in the courtroom. The artist Joseph Papin recorded one such scene in the drawing used here.
