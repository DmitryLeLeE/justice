On 12 February 1894 Émile Henry threw a bomb into the Café Terminus near the Gare Saint-Lazare in Paris, killing one person and wounding many others. He was arrested at the scene.

His trial before the Seine Assize Court opened in late April 1894. Henry did not deny the attack and gave a long statement presenting it as a political act. The press covered the hearing with sketches, among them the one from Le Matin shown here. He was condemned to death and executed less than a month later.
