North Korea captured the intelligence ship USS Pueblo in January 1968. The crew was released in December 1968 after long captivity.

In early 1969 a Navy court of inquiry met at Coronado, California, to establish what had happened and whether anyone should face discipline. The court recommended courts-martial for the ship's commander, Lloyd Bucher, and another officer, but the Navy Secretary dropped the matter and no one was tried. The painter Arnold Mesches made courtroom drawings of the hearing.
