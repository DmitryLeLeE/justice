## The case

Ten people arrested in the United States as Russian agents appeared before a New York court in June 2010.

They pleaded guilty on 28 June 2010 and were deported in an exchange of prisoners in Vienna.

## In the courtroom

The arrests ended a ten-year FBI investigation. The suspects had lived under false identities as ordinary families, and several had children who knew nothing. The charges were relatively light, since none had been caught passing classified material, and the case was resolved quickly.

The drawing dates from the hearing on 28 June.

## Aftermath

The four released by Russia included Sergei Skripal, who was poisoned in England in 2018. Chapman went on to a career on Russian television.
