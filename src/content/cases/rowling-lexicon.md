## The case

J. K. Rowling testified in a New York court against the publisher of an unofficial Harry Potter encyclopedia, the Lexicon.

The judge ruled for Rowling and Warner Bros. in September 2008 and barred publication of the book.

## In the courtroom

Rowling had said she wanted to write her own encyclopedia, and the Lexicon's publisher said its book was a reference work and a fair use of her material. She testified in April 2008. The judge found that the book copied too much of her words and ideas, and that its use was not transformative enough to be fair use, but he also said that reference guides to fiction are generally allowed.

## Aftermath

Statutory damages were $6,750, and the book as planned was barred. A revised version, with the offending passages cut, was published in 2009.
