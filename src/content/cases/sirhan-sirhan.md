Sirhan Sirhan was tried in Los Angeles for the shooting of Senator Robert F. Kennedy at the Ambassador Hotel in June 1968.

Sirhan was convicted of first-degree murder in April 1969 and sentenced to death. The sentence was commuted to life imprisonment in 1972.

The drawing shown is by Arnold Mesches, courtesy of the Library of Congress. The case file gives the main facts; see the sources for the full record.
