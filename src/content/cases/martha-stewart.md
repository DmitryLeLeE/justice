## The case

Martha Stewart was tried in New York for lying to investigators about her sale of ImClone shares. The drawing dates from 1 February 2004.

Stewart was convicted on 5 March 2004 and served five months in prison.

## In the courtroom

Stewart was not charged with insider trading. The government's case was about what she said to investigators afterwards, and the story that she and Bacanovic had a standing order to sell if the shares dropped to $60. The jury did not believe it. The judge dismissed the securities-fraud count during the trial, and the jury convicted her on the other four.

## Aftermath

She served her time at a federal prison in Alderson, West Virginia, and returned to the company she had founded. She said afterwards that the case had changed her view of the justice system. The drawing here shows her in court on 1 February 2004, during the first days of testimony.
