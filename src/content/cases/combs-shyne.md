## The case

Sean Combs and the rapper Shyne were tried in New York over a shooting at a Manhattan nightclub in December 1999.

Combs was acquitted of all charges on 16 March 2001. Shyne was convicted and sentenced to ten years.

## In the courtroom

The case arrived with a cast: a music mogul, his then-girlfriend Jennifer Lopez, a rapper he was promoting and a defence team that included Johnnie Cochran. Prosecutors said Combs had a pistol in the club and tried to bribe his driver to say it was his. The defence said Combs never had a gun and that the driver was a liar.

## Verdict

The jury acquitted Combs on every count. Shyne was convicted of assault and reckless endangerment and of gun possession, and was acquitted of the attempted murder counts. He was sentenced to ten years and, after his release, left for Belize.
