## The case

Judge Sonia Sotomayor heard the players' union case against the team owners during the 1994-95 baseball strike.

On 31 March 1995 she granted an injunction against the owners, and the strike ended.

## In the courtroom

The National Labor Relations Board had found cause to believe that the owners had broken the labor law by throwing out the old agreement. Sotomayor, then a district judge in Manhattan, heard the case in a single sitting and read her ruling from the bench in a packed courtroom.

She wrote that the owners had unilaterally changed the terms of the contract and ordered them to restore the old system. The appeals court refused to stay the order, the owners dropped their plan, and the shortened season began in late April.

## Aftermath

The ruling made her widely known. She was nominated to the Supreme Court by Barack Obama in 2009 and sworn in that August.
