## The case

In June 1929 Theora Hix, a student at Ohio State University, was found murdered. James Howard Snook, a professor of veterinary medicine and an Olympic gold medallist in shooting, was charged with the crime.

The Columbus Dispatch had the young cartoonist Milton Caniff draw the courtroom for its daily coverage. Snook was found guilty and went to the electric chair in Ohio in February 1930.

## The artist

Snook had won a gold medal in team pistol shooting at the 1920 Olympics, and his trial was one of the sensations of the year in Columbus. The Dispatch sent Milton Caniff, then in his early twenties, to draw the courtroom. Caniff went on to create "Terry and the Pirates" in 1934 and "Steve Canyon" in 1947, and the trial sketches are an early example of his line work.

## Aftermath

Snook went to the electric chair in February 1930.
