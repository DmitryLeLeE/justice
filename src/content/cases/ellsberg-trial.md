Daniel Ellsberg and Anthony Russo were tried in Los Angeles for leaking the Pentagon Papers. The drawing dates from 14 July 1972.

Judge William Byrne dismissed all charges on 11 May 1973 because of government misconduct, including the break-in at Ellsberg's psychiatrist's office.

The drawing shown is by Rose, courtesy of the Library of Congress. The case file gives the main facts; see the sources for the full record.
