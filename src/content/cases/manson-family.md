Charles Manson and three followers were tried in Los Angeles for the 1969 Tate and LaBianca murders. The drawing shows Manson leaping towards Judge Charles Older on 5 October 1970.

Manson and his co-defendants were convicted in January 1971. Their death sentences were later converted to life imprisonment.

The drawing shown is by Gary Robles, courtesy of the Library of Congress. The case file gives the main facts; see the sources for the full record.
