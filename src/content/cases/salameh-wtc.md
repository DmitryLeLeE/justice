Mohammed Salameh and other defendants were tried in New York for the February 1993 truck bombing of the World Trade Center.

Salameh and three others were convicted in March 1994. Each was sentenced to 240 years in prison.

The drawing shown is by Williams, courtesy of the Library of Congress. The case file gives the main facts; see the sources for the full record.
