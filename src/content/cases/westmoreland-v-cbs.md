## The case

General William Westmoreland sued CBS for libel over a documentary that accused him of misleading Washington about enemy strength in Vietnam.

The case ended in February 1985 when Westmoreland dropped the suit before the jury could rule.

## In the courtroom

The documentary said that in 1967 Westmoreland and his staff had held down the estimates of enemy troop strength to make it appear that the war was being won. Witnesses from the intelligence staff supported that claim, and the trial was a contest over what had been known and when. The general's lawyers also showed that CBS's own staff had questioned the program.

Westmoreland ended the suit shortly before the jury was to be sent out. In the joint statement CBS said it had not intended to say that he was unpatriotic or disloyal, and he said he had not meant to question the network's integrity.

## Aftermath

Neither side paid damages and each claimed a win. The trial remains a standard reference for press and libel law, and for the argument over the numbers in Vietnam.
