Jean Harris, headmistress of an exclusive girls' school, was tried in White Plains for the shooting of her lover, the cardiologist Herman Tarnower.

Harris was convicted of second-degree murder on 24 February 1981. Her sentence was commuted in 1992.

The drawing shown is by Joseph Papin, courtesy of the Library of Congress. The case file gives the main facts; see the sources for the full record.
