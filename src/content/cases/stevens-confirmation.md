## The case

The Senate Judiciary Committee questioned Judge John Paul Stevens in December 1975 on his nomination to the Supreme Court.

The Senate confirmed Stevens on 17 December 1975. He served until 2010.

## In the room

Stevens was a little-known appeals court judge from Chicago, and the hearing was calm. The senators asked him about his record on antitrust and civil rights, and what he said about the role of a judge.

## Aftermath

He was confirmed with no opposition. Appointed as a moderate Republican, he became the leader of the liberal wing of the Court. When he retired in 2010 he was the second-longest-serving justice in its history.
